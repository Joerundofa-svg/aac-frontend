Getting started

Dashboard gives you an overview of totals, recent activity, and fund balances.

Transactions is where giving, fundraising, and expenses are recorded. Click "Add transaction", fill in the details, and save.

Funds tracks money by purpose (e.g. Building Fund, Missions Fund) — each fund's balance updates automatically from the transactions tagged to it.

Provinces and Reports are visible to Admins and Bishops, showing contributions and summaries across the organization.

Something not working?

If a page won't load or shows an error, check that your administrator's server is running. If a save fails, a red message will appear at the bottom-right of the screen explaining why — for most errors, that message tells you exactly what to fix (e.g. a missing field or an expired session, in which case simply sign in again).
